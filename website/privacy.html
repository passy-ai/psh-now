Privacy and data

Last updated: October 2026

psh.now is operated by Absolum. The service handles one kind of object, the approval request, and the decision that comes back with it. This page lists what a request carries and what happens to it.

What a request carries

  • Approval requests: the title and message an agent sends, the command line when the agent includes it, and the approve, deny or answer that comes back.
  • Keys and pairing data: the key a request is posted with, the channel it belongs to, and the devices you pair, so the request can reach you.

Files and documents stay on your machine and are never part of a request. A request that expires or is cancelled costs nothing.

Where it is kept

A request and its decision are kept in the history of the channel it was sent to, so a member can read the decision later. Enterprise plans add a longer history.

Self-hosted, requests, keys and history stay in your own Postgres database behind a Bun service, and you decide how long they are kept. Nothing passes through this host.

What the service does not do

  • It sets no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • It sells no data and shares none of it for marketing.

What you control

An agent sends only the requests you route to psh.now. A key can be limited to one channel, so the agent can only ask there. A decision is made by a person signed in, or with the account key.

Contact

Questions about what is stored: email push@passy.ai, or reach Absolum at absolum.nl.
