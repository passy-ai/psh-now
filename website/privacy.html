Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

psh.now is operated by Absolum. We keep this short because we collect very little.

What we process

  • Approval requests - the short title and message an agent sends, and your approve/deny/answer.
  • Keys and pairing data - the channel key and the devices you pair, so a request can reach you.

That's it. psh.now only processes approval requests. We don't ask for or store your files, documents, or your agent's work.

Automatic deletion

Request data is deleted automatically once it is answered or times out. We don't keep a long-term record of your requests.

What we don't do

  • No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • No selling or sharing your data for marketing.

Your rules

You decide what each agent shares and how. Set your own rules through the install / evoke command when you connect an agent.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@psh.now or reach us at absolum.nl.
