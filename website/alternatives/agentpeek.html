An open source alternative to AgentPeek

AgentPeek is a command centre for coding agents on a Mac: every session in the notch, permission prompts answered with a keystroke, token spend on a gauge. psh.now answers the case it leaves open, where the person who has to say yes is not at that machine. Different surfaces, different jobs, and most teams could use both.

What AgentPeek is, in its own terms

Everything below is what AgentPeek publishes on agentpeek.app and in its docs, read on 11 November 2026. Their site is the source of truth if anything here goes stale.

A Mac app, nothing else

macOS 14 or newer, Apple silicon. The notch, the menu bar and a floating pill are the interface. There is no server to run and no account to create.

One-time purchase, one seat per Mac

$14.99 for one Mac, $24.99 for two, $34.99 for three. A free 3-day trial per machine, no subscription, and a seat is a physical Mac.

26 agents in one window

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Cline, Aider, Goose, Qwen, Kimi, Devin and the rest, each with a documented host matrix of what it can do.

More than approvals

Token usage and rate-limit windows with budget alerts, local dev servers, quick routes into configs, saved shell actions, widgets and an agent board.

Where the two differ

They overlap on one thing, answering an agent's permission prompt, and split on everything around it.

Pick AgentPeek when

You are at the Mac all day

Six parallel sessions in a notch, answered with a keystroke, is faster than switching to a browser tab and back. That is the whole point of it.

You want the spend view

Rate-limit windows and budget alerts per agent, in the same window as the approvals. psh.now has nothing like it.

A one-time price suits you

Fifteen dollars once, no account, no subscription, no server to keep alive. For a single developer on one Mac, that is hard to beat.

You want the whole toolkit

Session transcripts, follow-up prompts, dev servers, widgets on any display. It is a cockpit, not an approval endpoint.

Pick psh.now when

The approver is not at that Mac

A deploy at 2am, a question while you are on a train, a teammate covering for you. The dashboard is a URL, not a machine.

Several people share the on-call

One channel per project, anyone on it can answer, and the record says who did. No handover of a laptop required.

Agents run somewhere you do not sit

A CI runner, a cron job, a container on a server. It needs one HTTP call, not a Mac app watching the process.

You want to read and host the code

MIT, self-hosted, your Postgres, your mail. The approval path is yours to audit and change.

What psh.now does not do

The other side of the table, stated plainly.

  • No spend tracking. Not a token, not a limit, not a budget alert. psh.now records answers, not money.
  • No session monitoring. It never sees the agent's transcript, only the question the agent chose to ask.
  • No Mac notch. The surface is a web page, and the honest claim is a PWA on the home screen, with no push notifications behind it.
  • No local tooling. No dev-server list, no quick routes, no saved shell actions.

If you work at one Mac and want the cockpit, AgentPeek is the better buy. If the person who has to say yes might be somewhere else, that is what psh.now is for.
