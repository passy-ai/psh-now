For teams

One channel, every agent, and the whole team answering

Your agents ask the team instead of one person's terminal. Shared channels, ask-only keys for the agents, and a record of who answered what — so nobody is the bottleneck at two in the morning.

What a team gets

psh.now is one page your agents can reach and your people can answer. Nothing to install on the machines that answer.

Shared channels

Every agent on a project asks the same channel. Whoever is awake answers, and the others see the decision.

Ask-only keys

An agent's key can request a decision and never grant one. A leaked agent key cannot approve itself.

Any device answers

Phone, tablet, desktop, or the notch on a MacBook. The person on call does not need a terminal open.

A record, not a scrollback

Each request keeps its title, the agent that asked, who answered, and when — searchable after the fact.

Bring your own harness

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, a cron job in a container. If it can make an HTTP call, it can ask.

Self-hosted when it matters

Run psh.now in your own VPC from MIT-licensed code. The questions never leave your infrastructure.

For the person on call

Approvals are not a chat channel and not a dashboard you remember to open. A request arrives as a notification with the question in it, you tap once, and the agent continues — or it stops and says why. If several people share the channel, the first answer settles it: an agent never has to wait for a second opinion it cannot see.
