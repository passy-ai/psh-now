2. What psh.now does

psh.now holds one approval request open between an AI agent and a person. The agent sends an HTTP request describing the step it is about to take and the call stays open. Someone approves, denies or answers it in words from a phone, a browser or the macOS notch app, and the call returns with that decision. psh.now does not run your agents or your workloads, and the files they touch stay on your machine.

3. Accounts and keys

You can start without an account and without a card. Connecting an agent gives it a key that it sends in the x-psh-key header with each request. A key can be limited to one channel, so the agent can only ask there. Every decision is made by a person signed in or with the account key, and the record says who. Keep your keys secret.

4. Your data

A request carries a title, a message and, when the agent includes it, the command line. Requests and their decisions are kept in the workspace history. When you self-host, they stay in your own Postgres database and you decide how long they are kept. See the Privacy Policy.
