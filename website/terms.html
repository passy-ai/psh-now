2. What psh.now does

psh.now brokers approval requests between AI agents and people. An agent sends a short title and message; you (or someone you have authorized) approve, deny, or reply. psh.now does not run your agents or your workloads, and stores no files.

3. Accounts and keys

You can start without an account. Connecting an agent mints keys on your behalf: a channel key is ask-only and can never approve or manage the account, and an account key can manage it. Keep your keys secret.
