Pricing

psh.now charges for requests, one human decision each. Members are unlimited on every plan, storage is not metered, and the server is MIT licensed, so self-hosting costs nothing.

How usage is counted

An approval costs two requests: one inbound for the agent's ask, one outbound for the answer. A request that expires, is cancelled or goes unanswered costs nothing. Reads, polling and delivery to several devices are free.

Allowances are totals, not daily caps. Past the free allowance a token is spent when the workspace has one; otherwise the request is refused with 402 plan_limit, naming the plan, the usage and the limit. Every response carries a usage block: 

{
  "plan": "free",
  "requestsToday": 12,
  "limit": 100,
  "via": "allowance",
  "tokenBalance": 0
}

via is allowance, token or blocked.

What is not charged for

  • Members. Unlimited on every plan.
  • Devices and connections. As many agents and projects as you like.
  • Reads and polling. Only a decision costs requests.
  • Self-hosting. MIT licensed, no paid tier.
